Product 01

GHOSTWALL_x

A privacy firewall built in Rust, Tauri and Svelte. It recognizes AI crawlers by how they identify and behave, then drops them before they reach your content. Your human visitors never notice it's there.

Step 1 of 3 · Detect

Know every bot at the door.

GHOSTWALL_x matches incoming requests against known AI crawler signatures: user agents, network origins and request patterns that scrapers can't easily fake.

$ ghostwall inspect --live
GET /blog/post-14   UA GPTBot/1.2
  → match ai-crawler · openai
GET /about          UA Mozilla/5.0 (Mac)
  → match human · allow
Step 2 of 3 · Block

Stop it at the network layer.

Most bot blocking happens after your server has already done the work. GHOSTWALL_x drops the connection first, so no page is rendered and nothing is handed over.

DROP CCBot          tcp/443  0 bytes sent
DROP Bytespider     tcp/443  0 bytes sent
DROP PerplexityBot  tcp/443  0 bytes sent
PASS Safari/17.4     tcp/443  served
Step 3 of 3 · Vanish

Stay off the training set.

Rules update as new crawlers appear. You see every blocked request in one place, and your work stays yours instead of quietly becoming someone else's model.

ghostwall status
rules        up to date
blocked 24h  1,284
leaked       0
mode         invisible
